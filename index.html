You send the documents. We handle everything between your front door and Tráfico.

Deadlines

The clock starts when you become resident.

Talk to us early. Some exemptions are only open for a few weeks.

30 days

To register as a resident

A resident's foreign-plated car is expected to be registered here within 30 days of starting to use it in Spain, or 60 days if you moved your home here with it.

60 days

To claim the registration-tax exemption

People moving their home to Spain can be exempt from registration tax, but the claim has to be filed within this window. Miss it and you may lose the exemption.

12 months

To claim customs relief on a UK car

Duty and import IVA are waived if you lived outside the EU for at least 12 months, owned and used the car for six months before moving, and claim within 12 months of moving.

50–150%

Penalties on unpaid tax

In recent Guardia Civil and tax agency checks on foreign-plated cars in the Balearics, penalties ran at 50–150% of the unpaid tax, and cars can be immobilised on the spot.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to bring the car to you?

No. We collect from home or work across Barcelona, the Garraf and the Penedès, and can arrange collection from elsewhere in Catalonia. If you'd rather drop the car off, that works too.

Can I keep driving on UK plates while you work on it?

Yes, as long as the car stays legal in the UK, with road tax, MOT and insurance, and you're within the time Spanish rules allow. We'll tell you where you stand when we quote.

What happens with my headlights?

UK headlights dip towards the left-hand kerb, which dazzles oncoming drivers here, so the import ITV checks for a beam pattern made for driving on the right. Depending on the lamp, we fit E-marked units built for right-hand traffic or have your existing lamps adjusted.

Send us a photo of one headlight and the label on top of it and we'll price it.

Will I pay customs and IVA on a UK car?

Usually not, if you're moving your home to Spain. The conditions are that you lived outside the EU for at least 12 months, owned and used the car for six months before moving, and claim within 12 months of arriving.

Otherwise UK cars pay 10% duty plus 21% import IVA on their value, although cars built in the UK can qualify for 0% duty with proof of origin. We check this before you commit to anything.

How long does it take?

Usually two to four weeks from receiving your documents to new plates on the car. Registration typically completes about 10 working days after the ITV pass. A customs claim or special-order headlights can add time.

What if the car fails the ITV?

The inspector lists what needs fixing. We tell you the price of any extra work before doing it, fix it, and take the car back for the re-test.

Do you only handle UK cars?

No. We also register Irish and other EU cars, and can advise on motorhomes, motorbikes and cars from outside the EU.

Can you help with my driving licence and insurance?

Yes. Ask us about exchanging a UK licence for a Spanish one, and about insurance on Spanish plates, when you request your quote.

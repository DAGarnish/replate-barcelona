Bringing your UK car with you when you move to Spain makes sense: you know the car, it's paid for, and buying a new one here isn't cheap. But the moment you become resident, the clock starts on several deadlines. Miss them and you can lose valuable tax exemptions, or face fines.

Here's what you need to know if you're moving to Catalonia with a UK car. The figures below are the 2026 rules.

## The four numbers that matter

| Deadline | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **30 or 60 days** | Registering the car on Spanish plates |
| **60 days** | Claiming the registration-tax exemption |
| **12 months** | Claiming customs relief on a UK car |
| **50–150%** | Penalties seen on unpaid tax in recent checks |

## 30 or 60 days: registering the car

Once you live in Spain, your car is expected to be on Spanish plates. A resident's foreign-plated car should be registered here within **30 days** of starting to use it in Spain, or **60 days** if you moved your home here with it.

You can usually keep driving on UK plates while the work is done, as long as the car stays legal in the UK, with road tax, an MOT and insurance, and you're within the time the Spanish rules allow.

## 60 days: the registration-tax exemption

Spanish registration tax (IEDMT) is a percentage of the official value of your car, set by its CO₂ figure, which you'll find in box V.7 of your V5C:

- **0%** up to 120 g/km
- **4.75%** for 121–159 g/km
- **9.75%** for 160–199 g/km
- **16%** for 200 g/km and over (Catalonia sets its own rate on the top band)

People moving their home to Spain **can be exempt** from this tax, but the claim has to be filed within the 60-day window. For a higher-emission car, that exemption can be worth a lot of money. **Miss the window and you may lose it.**

## 12 months: customs relief on a UK car

Since Brexit, the UK is outside the EU, so UK cars are technically imports. Without relief, a UK car pays **10% customs duty plus 21% import IVA** on its value.

But if you're moving your home to Spain, duty and import IVA are **waived** when:

1. You lived outside the EU for at least **12 months** before moving.
2. You owned and used the car for at least **six months** before moving.
3. You claim within **12 months** of moving.

Cars built in the UK can also qualify for 0% duty with proof of origin.

## What happens if you leave it

It's tempting to keep driving on UK plates and deal with it later. Authorities do check. In recent Guardia Civil and tax agency checks on foreign-plated cars in the Balearics, penalties ran at **50–150% of the unpaid tax**, and cars can be immobilised on the spot.

## What you'll need

Get these together early and the process moves quickly:

- Your original **V5C** logbook
- **Passport**
- **NIE** certificate or **TIE** residence card
- **Padrón** certificate from your town hall, ideally issued in the last three months, or your deeds or rental contract
- **Driving licence**
- Photos of the car from all four sides, and of the VIN plate, weights plate and tyre pressure sticker
- **Certificate of Conformity**, if you have one (it can be ordered if not)
- Valid **insurance** until the new plates go on
